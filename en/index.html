How the calculation works

Monthly profit = (price − variable cost − price × percentage fee − flat fee) × sales volume − monthly fixed costs. The sales volume needed to preserve current profit is rounded up to the next whole unit when the proposed per-sale contribution is positive.

Important: This calculator does not predict how customers will respond to a price change. It does not include taxes, inventory valuation, financing, or other costs unless you include them in your inputs. It is not accounting or financial advice.
